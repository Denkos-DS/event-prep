# Chalet weekend — August 2026

The event this skill was built from. Included unedited, including the mistakes
that were caught along the way.

**Shape:** 33 guests (26 regular, 7 vegan) · Friday evening to Monday morning ·
multiple chalets · three cooked meals, three breakfasts · $2,850 total budget
across food and drinks.

**Constraints that drove everything:**
- Arrival around 7 PM Friday → no Friday breakfast, late first dinner
- Music curfew at 11 PM → party starts early → main meals at 3:00 PM
- Multiple buildings → oven and fridge capacity split across kitchens
- A grocery store near the venue → staples could be bought lean

**Files**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `guest-schedule.html` | the one-pager sent to all 33 guests |
| `planning-doc.html` | the full plan — recipes, timelines, budget, logistics |
| `planning-doc.md` | same, as markdown for collaborative editing |
| `shopping-list.html` | store-filtered tick-off list with running totals |

**Errors caught while building it**, all now in the skill's audit checklist:

- Breakfast quantities in the original plan were per-day figures that had never
  been multiplied for a four-morning stay
- 5 kg of bone-in chicken was treated as 5 kg of meat
- Salt, pepper, cooking oil, cabbage, BBQ sauce, garlic and onion powder appeared
  in recipes and on no shopping list
- Seven vegan guests had nothing to eat at breakfast
- Shish taouk was listed with lemon wedges instead of toum
- The alcohol line was a $1,600 placeholder against a real cost near $800 — the
  slack covered the entire food shortfall
